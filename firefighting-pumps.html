Firefighting Pumping Solutions

Pumping solutions for fire hydrant and fire protection systems used in commercial, residential, industrial, and infrastructure projects.

We supply pump sets suited to fire hydrant and fire protection systems. Share your system requirements and we will help you select a suitable configuration.

Main Fire Pumps

Primary pumps that supply water to fire hydrant and sprinkler systems. Selected to match the system's design duty.

Typical applications: Fire hydrant systems, sprinkler systems, commercial and industrial buildings.

Send Enquiry

Diesel Fire Pumps

Engine-driven fire pumps used where a standby or non-electric drive is required for the fire system.

Typical applications: Standby fire duty, sites requiring engine-driven backup.

Send Enquiry

Jockey Pumps

Pressure-maintenance pumps that hold system pressure and reduce main-pump starts.

Typical applications: Pressure maintenance in hydrant and sprinkler lines.

Send Enquiry

Fire Hydrant Control Panels

Control panels for starting, monitoring and managing fire pump operation.

Typical applications: Control and monitoring of fire pump sets.
